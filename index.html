Your Halloween finishing-touch checklist

1.

Give your costume a test run

Sit, walk, and move around in it. Check how masks, wigs, hats, and collars sit around your jewelry, and watch for anything that could catch.

2.

Pick one finishing touch

A simple costume can come together with one small detail.
Choose a piece that fits your style.

3.

Check the fit before choosing

Check your gauge and length—or hoop diameter—against the jewelry you already wear. A similar-looking piece can be a different size.
